About

Who I am, in plain language

I'm a senior software engineer based in Cairo. I spent the last eight years building and operating real products end to end, with backend and platform as my home ground: Ruby on Rails, Node.js and Go, with the AWS infrastructure underneath.

Most of my work is the unglamorous kind that keeps a product standing up: keeping databases out of CPU saturation, replacing autoscaling that kills long jobs, and making an LLM engine fail over cleanly when a provider degrades. I like owning a system from the architecture decision to the 3am incident.

I've worked on a meal-subscription platform serving 100,000 daily users, a US health product, and the AI translation platform behind the product. I'm looking for a senior backend or platform role where I can own systems, and I'm happy to work remotely.
